## 📌 Project Objective  
The objective of this project is to analyze **global energy consumption data using SQL**, compare usage patterns across countries, and generate insights on:  
- **Trends** in energy demand and supply.  
- **Emissions** and their relation to GDP and population.  
- **Efficiency** of energy use across countries.  
- Support **policy-making & sustainable energy management**.  

---

## 🗂️ Database Schema (ERD)  
- **Country** – Central table connecting all others.  
- **Emission, Consumption, Production** – Store energy and environmental data.  
- **GDP** – Yearly economic values of each country.  
- **Population** – Yearly population data.  
- All tables are linked by **country** and **year** for easy comparison.

## 🔎 Key SQL Queries & Analysis  
1. Total emissions per country (latest year).  
2. Top 5 countries by GDP.  
3. Compare energy production vs. consumption by year and country.  
4. Energy types contributing most to emissions (fossil fuels vs. renewables).  
5. Year-over-year change in global emissions.  
6. GDP trends across countries.  
7. Impact of population growth on emissions.  
8. Energy consumption trends in major economies.  
9. Yearly change in emissions per capita.  
10. Emission-to-GDP ratio by country.  
11. Energy consumption per capita (last decade).  
12. Energy production per capita across countries.  
13. Countries with highest energy consumption relative to GDP.  
14. Top 10 countries by population vs. emissions.  
15. Global share (%) of emissions by country.  
16. Global average GDP, emissions, and population by year.  

---

## 📊 Final Business Insights  
- High emissions are directly linked with top energy-consuming countries.  
- **GDP growth** strongly correlates with **energy production & consumption**.  
- **Per capita analysis** shows unequal energy and emission distribution.  
- Major economies **drive global energy & emission trends**.  

---

## 💡 Key Recommendations  
- **Invest in renewable energy** to reduce dependency on fossil fuels.  
- Implement **energy efficiency policies** to lower emissions without slowing GDP growth.  
- Focus on **sustainable development strategies** in major economies.  
- Monitor **per capita trends** to ensure fair & balanced global energy use.
